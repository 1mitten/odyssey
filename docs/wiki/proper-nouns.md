# Proper nouns

Names that are not interface keys: people, places, factions, creatures, the calendar. The brief requires every one of these to be ours, so this page exists to make the empty rows visible.

| The world | **Carrow** | proposed  | A city-world: one settlement that grew until it covered the planet. Short, bleak, easy to say, and it survives being said a thousand times. The register throughout is English industrial rather than classical or invented-exotic: a working future, not a mythic one |
| The city | **Ashgate** | proposed  | The district the prototype is set in. Formally Ashgate Division, colloquially the Gate. Ash for what happened to it, gate because a division boundary is a gate.
