What to review, in order

1. The visibility modes. ADR 0003 settled x-ray as the default. This mockup exists to let you change your mind cheaply. Move the slice on the depth ruler, then walk the Above control through all six positions. Roofs off is the one that was never on the original list of three, and it is what the concept renders actually depict.

2. The tuning numbers. The x-ray falloff was tuned when x-ray was the least-favoured option, so it starts too faint to be a default. Drag near alpha and falloff until a forty-layer tower reads without turning to soup, then copy the printed values into docs/design/09-ui-and-input.md. Numbers chosen by eye beat numbers chosen in prose.

3. Icon coverage. Every slot here is driven by docs/design/icon-map.csv. A slot outlined in magenta has no art in your sheets. The coverage panel counts them. Switch Icons to Keys to see what each slot asks for, or Text to check that the interface still reads with no pictures at all.

4. Icon size. ADR 0004 moved the display sizes to 32 and 64 pixels, because pixel art at a fractional scale shimmers. The Size control shows both. Check the ledger and the roster bar: those are the densest regions and the ones the change costs most.

Once the eight sheets are in art-source/icons/sheets/ and the row and column columns of the mapping are filled in, the placeholders here become the real art with no further work: the slots address the sheets directly as sprites.
